方七 · 腹脹方 · Routine 7 of 14 · about 10 min

Bloating, a point at a time

5 points in order: Zhongwan, Tianshu, Qihai, Zusanli, Neiguan. First sit somewhere supported, drop the shoulders, unclench the jaw and take a few slow breaths.

About Bloating →
五步

5 steps

Before you startWarm hands help. Press only as firmly as feels pleasant, and let go slowly. Once or twice a day is plenty.
經穴圖 · 按腹脹方方
第一步 · Step 1 of 5中脘ZhongwanCV12
Where
On the midline of the upper belly, halfway between the navel and the lower tip of the breastbone.
How
Flat fingers halfway between navel and breastbone; sink in slowly on each breath out, light to moderate, for about a minute.
How to find Zhongwan →
Side
One point, on the midline.
1:00ready
Twelve marks, one press. Breathe out as you press, ease as you breathe in.

Safety

停

Stop if it hurts

A dull, heavy ache is right. Sharp, electric or spreading pain means stop at once. Leave broken, inflamed or numb skin alone.

孕

If you are pregnant

Talk with a qualified practitioner before starting any self-pressure routine.

急

This is not the moment

Leave the points for now if you are a woman of 50 or over and bloating persists or keeps coming back: ask about ovarian checks; there is weight loss, blood in the stool or black stool, or anaemia; or swallowing is hard, or vomiting keeps coming back.
