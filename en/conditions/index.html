急

When to get help now

Leave the points for later and see a doctor promptly if you have

  • a sudden, severe headache unlike any before, or one after a blow to the head
  • fever with a stiff neck, or new weakness, numbness, confusion or trouble seeing
  • chest pain, vomit with blood in it, or vomiting that keeps fluids from staying down
  • severe belly pain when you could be pregnant
  • a face that droops, an arm that weakens or speech that slurs, all of a sudden
  • new swelling, warmth or redness in one arm or leg, or sudden breathlessness
  • loss of bladder or bowel control, or numbness around the groin
  • any thought of harming yourself

Call your local emergency number, or see a doctor today. Urgency is information; let a professional read it.

方法

How a set works

Press a set with me · 6 min
  1. 一
    尋

    Find

    Each point sits by a landmark: a bone, a crease, a hollow. Find it by feel, not by force. A dull, heavy ache tells you that you are there.

  2. 二
    按

    Press

    Thumb or fingertip pad, only as firm as feels pleasant. Hold for five slow breaths, about a minute, then let go slowly.

  3. 三
    息

    Pause

    Move to the next point in order. Once or twice a day is plenty. Sharp, electric or spreading pain means stop.

Fingers and palms only, never needles. Skip broken or irritated skin. Pregnant, unwell or on regular medication? Ask a qualified practitioner first.
