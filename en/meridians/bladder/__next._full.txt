4a:T40a,The counting is friendlier than it sounds. Bend the head forward: the bump that leads at the base of the neck caps the seventh cervical vertebra, and the station just below it is dazhu (BL11). Follow a shoulder blade to its lower angle: that landmark rides level with the seventh thoracic vertebra and geshu (BL17). Rest your hands on your waist with thumbs pointing backwards: the tops of the hip crests mark roughly the fourth lumbar vertebra, where dachangshu (BL25) waits.
