合谷

Hegu LI4 (LI4), 合谷

“Joining Valley” · LI4Large Intestine · 4th of 20

On the back of the hand, on the mound between thumb and index finger when the thumb presses in.

Where.
On the back of the hand, on the mound between thumb and index finger when the thumb presses in.
For.
Headache; pain of the face and teeth.
Press.
Toward the index-finger bone, one minute, then the other hand.
Caution.
Not in pregnancy.

LI4 is the 'command point of the head and face' — a classical daily choice when no term-specific entry applies.
